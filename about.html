Our Roots & Ambition

THE OKIO MANIFESTO

Crafted in India for modern high-output minds. Built on the belief that everyday drinks should be clean, functional, and beautifully designed.

01 / The Rebellion

NOT ANOTHER ORDINARY SOFT DRINK.

For decades, the beverage landscape in India was caught between two extremes. On one side were legacy sugary sodas loaded with corn syrup and harsh carbonation. On the other were antiquated health drinks wrapped in clichéd wellness tropes.

We created OKIO as a design-led functional alternative. We wanted a can that felt right at home on a design studio desk in Indiranagar, in a gym bag in Bandra, or at an evening gathering with friends.

A beverage that provides calm alertness, sustained physical hydration, and zero digestive bloat.

02 / What We Believe

THE OKIO CODE

01 / Aesthetic

Design as Respect

We respect the intelligence of our drinkers. No screaming neon fonts, no esports clichés, and no pseudo-scientific gimmicks. Pure, understated luxury.

02 / Science

Measured Precision

75mg organic green tea caffeine paired with 100mg L-theanine. Clinically substantiated ratios that support cognitive flow without anxiety.

03 / Origin

Modern India

Formulated, blended, and packaged in India to the most rigorous international standards. Crafted for the creators driving India's new era.

04 / Transparency

Zero Hidden Fillers

Every milligram is disclosed on our packaging. 0g added sugar, zero artificial colors, zero synthetic preservatives.
