01 / Formulation & Biology

The Functional Science

Standard energy drinks rely on synthetic petroleum-derived caffeine (160mg+), high taurine, and harsh carbonation with 30-40g of refined sugar. OKIO uses 75mg of clean plant caffeine from green tea extract paired with 100mg of L-theanine in a non-carbonated spring water base with 0g added sugar. You experience calm, steady focus without jittery tachycardia or digestive bloating.

Carbonation traps gas in the stomach, delays cellular hydration absorption, and causes acid reflux during workouts or focus sprints. By keeping OKIO non-carbonated, the fluid hydrates your cells immediately, feels exceptionally smooth, and is easy to drink cold or room temperature.

L-Theanine is a natural amino acid found in green tea leaves. It crosses the blood-brain barrier and stimulates alpha brainwave generation. When paired with natural caffeine, it smoothens out the energetic lift, dampens nervous jitters, and fosters prolonged mental flow.

03 / Storage & Ritual

Optimal Consumption

OKIO cans have a 12-month shelf life from the date of manufacture. Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. For the ultimate tasting experience, chill in the refrigerator before drinking.

Each can contains 75mg of clean caffeine (about the same as a single espresso). We recommend 1 to 2 cans per day to match your daily workflow rhythms.
