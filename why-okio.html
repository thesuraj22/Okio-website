01 / Formulation Science

THE OKIO STANDARD

We stripped away the synthetic taurine, the gas bloating, and the 40 grams of sugar. What remains is pure, calculated refreshment.

Energy Metabolism

SUSTAINED FLOW
VS. CHEMICAL CRASH

Standard energy drinks dump 160mg to 200mg of synthetic, anhydrous caffeine into your stomach. This produces an immediate spike in cortisol and epinephrine, causing accelerated heart rate and sweaty palms, followed by an unavoidable crash 90 minutes later.

OKIO pairs 75mg of slow-release caffeine derived from natural green tea extract with 100mg of L-theanine. This 1:1.3 ratio promotes alpha brainwave synchronization — creating a plateau of calm, uninterrupted concentration for 4 to 5 hours.
